FAQ / Help

How does mining work? +
MinePulse uses your device's idle CPU to mine Monero (XMR) through WebAssembly. You earn ₹70 daily once the target is reached.
Why is there a daily target? +
To ensure consistent earnings for all users and sustainable platform growth.
How do payouts work? +
Every Sunday, we send your accumulated earnings to your chosen payout method (USDT or UPI).
How do I reset my password? +
Please contact our support team at support@minepulse.com or via Telegram @MinePulseSupport.
